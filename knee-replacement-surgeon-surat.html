Knee Replacement Surgeon in Surat

Knee replacement may be considered when advanced knee arthritis causes persistent pain, stiffness, reduced walking tolerance, difficulty with stairs or major limitation of daily activities despite appropriate non-surgical treatment.

When Should You Consider an Evaluation?

  • Advanced knee osteoarthritis
  • Persistent pain at rest or during daily activity
  • Progressive stiffness or deformity
  • Difficulty walking, climbing stairs or standing
  • Symptoms affecting sleep, independence or quality of life

How the Joint Is Evaluated

  • Clinical examination and standing knee X-rays
  • Review of previous medicines, physiotherapy and injections
  • Assessment of alignment, movement and ligament stability
  • Medical fitness evaluation before surgery when planned

Treatment & Surgical Planning

  • Activity modification and weight management where appropriate
  • Medicines and physiotherapy
  • Selected injections when clinically appropriate
  • Total knee replacement for selected advanced arthritis
  • Partial knee replacement for selected disease patterns
  • Post-operative physiotherapy and rehabilitation
Important: Surgery is not required for every patient. The decision is individualized after clinical examination, imaging, overall health and response to appropriate non-surgical treatment are considered.

Knee Replacement Surgeon in Surat – Frequently Asked Questions

Does every patient with knee arthritis need replacement?

No. Many patients can be managed without surgery. Replacement is considered after symptoms, examination, X-rays, function and response to non-surgical treatment are reviewed.

When is knee replacement usually considered?

It may be considered when advanced arthritis causes persistent pain and functional limitation despite appropriate conservative care.

What is total knee replacement?

Damaged joint surfaces are replaced with prosthetic components to create a smoother, more functional joint surface.

What is partial knee replacement?

In selected patients with arthritis limited to one compartment, only the affected part of the knee may be replaced.

When can walking start after knee replacement?

Mobilization commonly begins early under the treating team and physiotherapist, but timing varies with the patient and procedure.

How long does recovery take?

Recovery is gradual and varies with age, health, pre-operative function, surgery and rehabilitation.

Will I need physiotherapy?

Rehabilitation is an important part of regaining movement, strength, balance and confidence after surgery.

How long does a knee implant last?

Implant longevity varies with implant, activity, body weight, alignment and other factors; no implant has a guaranteed lifespan.

Can both knees be replaced?

Some patients may be candidates for staged or simultaneous procedures, but the decision requires individualized medical and surgical assessment.

When can I use stairs or drive?

These activities are resumed according to strength, movement, pain control and medical advice; timelines vary.

What are the risks?

Potential risks include infection, blood clots, stiffness, persistent pain, implant-related problems and medical complications. Individual risks should be discussed before surgery.

Should I bring old X-rays?

Yes. Previous X-rays, MRI scans, prescriptions and treatment records can help assess progression and prior treatment.
