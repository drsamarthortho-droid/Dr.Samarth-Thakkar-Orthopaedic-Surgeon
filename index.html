Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consult an orthopaedic doctor?

Persistent bone, joint or back pain, swelling, reduced movement, difficulty walking, pain after injury, deformity, instability, numbness or weakness may warrant assessment.

Do you treat knee and hip arthritis?

Yes. Evaluation includes symptoms, examination and imaging when required, followed by an individualized plan ranging from non-surgical care to joint replacement evaluation when appropriate.

Do you provide knee replacement consultation in Surat?

Yes. Patients with advanced knee arthritis can be assessed for total or partial knee replacement when clinically appropriate.

Do you provide hip replacement consultation in Surat?

Yes. Patients with advanced hip arthritis, selected AVN cases and other severe hip-joint damage can be evaluated for hip replacement when appropriate.

Does every fracture need surgery?

No. Fracture treatment depends on bone, fracture pattern, displacement, stability, soft-tissue injury, age and activity. Options may include splint/cast, reduction or surgery.

Can sports injuries be treated without surgery?

Many sports injuries improve with activity modification, rehabilitation, bracing or other non-surgical treatment. Significant ligament, tendon or cartilage injuries may need further intervention.

What should I bring to consultation?

Bring previous X-rays, MRI/CT scans, prescriptions, medical records and a list of current medicines.

Can I come for a second opinion?

Yes. Previous reports and images are useful when reviewing diagnosis, treatment options or a proposed operation.
