Frequently Asked Questions

Does every fracture require surgery?

No. Depending on the fracture, treatment may involve a splint, cast, brace, reduction or surgery.

Does every patient with knee arthritis need knee replacement?

No. Treatment depends on pain, function, examination, X-rays and response to appropriate non-surgical treatment.

Does every ACL or meniscus injury require surgery?

No. Treatment depends on the type of injury, symptoms, instability, activity level and associated damage.

When should an injury be assessed urgently?

Severe pain, deformity, inability to bear weight, marked swelling, numbness or an open wound after an injury warrants prompt medical assessment.
