Orthopaedic Services – FAQs

Do you treat all orthopaedic conditions listed on this page?

The clinic provides orthopaedic assessment and treatment planning across common bone, joint, spine, fracture and sports conditions. Some conditions may require imaging, hospital-based procedures, another subspecialist or multidisciplinary referral.

Do all orthopaedic problems need surgery?

No. Many conditions are managed with education, activity modification, medicines, physiotherapy, supports or selected procedures. Surgery is recommended only when clinically indicated.

Can I consult for a second opinion?

Yes. Bring your previous X-rays, scans, reports, prescriptions and operative notes if available.

Do you evaluate knee and hip replacement patients?

Yes. Patients with advanced knee or hip arthritis can be evaluated for non-surgical care and, when appropriate, knee or hip replacement planning.

What should I bring for a fracture or sports injury?

Bring available X-rays/MRI, emergency records, prescriptions and details of the injury. For a new significant injury, timely assessment is important.

When should an injury be seen urgently?

Severe pain, deformity, inability to bear weight, an open wound, rapidly increasing swelling, fever, new numbness or weakness, or a cold/pale limb warrants prompt medical assessment.
