Understanding the Problem

Wrist and hand symptoms may be caused by fractures, tendon disorders, nerve compression such as carpal tunnel syndrome, trigger finger or arthritis.

How Is It Evaluated?

Assessment starts with your symptoms, medical history and a focused physical examination. X-rays, MRI, blood tests or other investigations are advised only when clinically appropriate.

Treatment Options

Treatment depends on the diagnosis and severity. Options may include activity modification, appropriate medicines, physiotherapy and rehabilitation, braces or supports, injections or surgery when indicated. Not every patient requires a procedure or operation.

When to seek prompt medical attention

Seek timely assessment after significant injury or if there is severe pain, deformity, inability to bear weight, rapidly increasing swelling, an open wound, fever, new numbness or weakness.
