Joint Replacement Surgeon in Surat

Joint replacement evaluation focuses on patients with advanced knee or hip arthritis and significant pain or mobility limitation. The aim is to determine whether continued non-surgical care or replacement surgery is appropriate.

Call for AppointmentWhatsApp Clinic

When Should You Consider an Evaluation?

  • Severe knee or hip pain
  • Difficulty walking, standing or using stairs
  • Progressive stiffness or deformity
  • Reduced independence in daily activities
  • Advanced arthritis on imaging

How the Joint Is Evaluated

  • Clinical examination and functional assessment
  • Standing knee or pelvis/hip X-rays as appropriate
  • Review of previous treatment and imaging
  • Medical fitness assessment when surgery is planned

Treatment & Surgical Planning

  • Non-surgical arthritis care when appropriate
  • Total or partial knee replacement in selected patients
  • Total hip replacement in selected patients
  • Pre-operative counselling and planning
  • Post-operative rehabilitation and follow-up
Important: Surgery is not required for every patient. The decision is individualized after clinical examination, imaging, overall health and response to appropriate non-surgical treatment are considered.

Joint Replacement Surgeon in Surat – Frequently Asked Questions

What is joint replacement?

Joint replacement is an operation in which damaged joint surfaces are replaced with prosthetic components. Knee and hip are the most commonly replaced major joints.

Who may be considered for joint replacement?

Patients with advanced joint damage, persistent pain and major functional limitation despite appropriate non-surgical treatment may be considered after evaluation.

Is surgery the first treatment for arthritis?

Usually not. Treatment depends on severity and may include activity modification, medicines, physiotherapy, weight management where appropriate and selected injections.

How is the decision made?

Symptoms, examination, X-rays, function, overall health, goals and response to previous treatment are considered together.

What should I bring?

Bring previous X-rays, MRI scans, prescriptions, medical records and a list of current medicines.
